Urban Cartography

I made this map to accompany a story about urban wildlife in cities across the United States. I decided the clearest way to tell this broad story was to zoom into a single animal that exemplifies the struggle to find space to roam. Naturally, I had to go with a dark style to match the tone of the topic and photos. See the digital version here.
